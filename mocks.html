Special notes:

  • English Literature: the papers contain questions on every set text; Aikya answers only Macbeth, An Inspector Calls, Jekyll and Hyde and her poetry collection.
  • History before 2025: older papers use the old formats. Adapt them: turn "Describe two features" (4) into two 2-mark "Describe one feature" questions; on Paper 2 use only one of the two consequences per sub-question (4 each); on Paper 3 offer a choice of two "Explain why" questions. From R3 use the 2025 sample papers and the June 2025 papers, which use the new format.
  • AQA and Pearson websites keep only recent years publicly; PMT hosts the older ones. The most recent 12 months are locked to teachers, which is why June 2026 papers aren't used.
  • 3D Design has no paper to mock: instead, from February, give her a 5-hour timed studio session each half-term to rehearse the 10-hour Externally Set Assignment.

Parent's guide: running and marking a mock

A mock only tells you something if it feels like the real thing and is marked strictly against the official mark scheme. Allow 2 hours for each Maths or Science paper, including marking.

Before (the evening before):

  1. Download the question paper, mark scheme and, where available, the examiner report; print the question paper single-sided.
  2. Do not let Aikya see the paper; check she hasn't already done it at school.
  3. Prepare: black pens, pencil, ruler, protractor, compasses, scientific calculator (not for Maths P1), clock in view, water.

During:

  1. Quiet room, phone in another room, no notes.
  2. Read out the start time and the finish time; give a 10-minute warning.
  3. Stop exactly on time. Unfinished questions are information, not failure.

Marking (same day if possible):

  1. Mark with the official mark scheme only. Award a mark only if the mark scheme says so; in Maths, method marks (M1) are given for correct working even when the answer is wrong.
  2. For English and History, read the level descriptors first, decide the level, then the mark within it; when unsure, choose the lower mark and ask her teacher to second-mark one essay a month.
  3. Convert the total to a grade with that paper's boundaries: Pearson grade boundaries · AQA results and boundaries · Maths Genie grade calculator. Boundaries apply to the whole qualification, so treat a single-paper grade as an indication.
  4. With Aikya, go through every lost mark (15 minutes): she writes each one in the Mistakes Log on the Plan page with its cause.

Readiness thresholds (percentage of marks on the paper):

Round On track for a 9 Watch Act now
R1 (Nov) 70%+ 55–69% Under 55%
R2 (Feb) 78%+ 65–77% Under 65%
R3 (Mar) 82%+ 70–81% Under 70%
R4 (Easter) 85%+ 75–84% Under 75%
R5 (final) 88%+ 80–87% Under 80%

These are planning targets set comfortably above typical grade 9 boundaries, not the boundaries themselves. "Act now" means: book a meeting with that subject teacher and move two extra study blocks a week to that subject until the next mock.
